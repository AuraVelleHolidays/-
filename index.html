Char Dham Yatra

9 Nights / 10 Days · Haridwar to Haridwar
Day 1–2

Arrive Haridwar, drive to Barkot for Yamunotri darshan.

Day 3–4

Barkot to Uttarkashi, Gangotri darshan and sacred holy bath.

Day 5–7

Drive to Sonprayag/Gaurikund, Kedarnath trek/pony, darshan & overnight stay.

Day 8–9

Descend to Sonprayag, drive via Rudraprayag to Badrinath & Mana village.

Day 10

Return drive to Rishikesh & Haridwar for final departure.

Inclusions

  • Comfortable private vehicle for complete circuit
  • Hotel stay on twin/triple sharing
  • Daily breakfast & dinner
  • Yatra registration & permits

Exclusions

  • Pony / palki / helicopter charges
  • Personal expenses & lunches

Do Dham Yatra

5 Nights / 6 Days · Haridwar to Haridwar
Day 1

Pick up from Haridwar, drive to Guptkashi / Sonprayag.

Day 2–3

Trek / pony to Kedarnath Temple, evening Aarti & darshan, return to Sonprayag.

Day 4–5

Drive to Badrinath via Chopta, holy bath at Tapt Kund & Badrinath Darshan.

Day 6

Return drive via Devprayag back to Haridwar.

Inclusions

  • Private vehicle (Haridwar to Haridwar)
  • Hotel stay & meal plan
  • Driver allowances & toll charges

Exclusions

  • Helicopter / pony tickets
  • Personal extra meals

Deoria Tall, Tungnath, Chandrashila & Kartik Swami Mandir

4 Nights / 5 Days · Ex-Rishikesh
Day 1

Drive from Rishikesh to Sari Village, short trek to Deoria Tall Lake & camp stay.

Day 2

Trek down to Sari, drive to Chopta, trek to Tungnath Temple & Chandrashila Peak.

Day 3

Drive from Chopta to Rudraprayag / Pokhari area, evening relaxation.

Day 4

Trek to Kartik Swami Mandir, panoramic 360-degree Himalayan views.

Day 5

Return drive to Rishikesh with memories of a lifetime.

Inclusions

  • Camping / Guesthouse stays
  • Trekking guide & equipment
  • Breakfast & dinner
  • Transfers from Rishikesh

Exclusions

  • Personal trekking gear
  • Unscheduled extra stays
